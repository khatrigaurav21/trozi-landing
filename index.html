Your data

What happens to your CV

Plain answers, so you can decide before you sign up.

What we keep

Your CV as text (not the original file), the jobs you add, and the CVs, cover letters and answers Trozi writes for you.

Where it's kept

In a database in Singapore. Generated PDFs have long, random web addresses. Anyone who has an address can open that file, so only share it with people you mean to.

Who processes it

Google's Gemini AI, on its paid tier, writes your documents. Google says it doesn't use that content to improve its products, and may keep it briefly to detect abuse.

Who can see it

Trozi is run by one person, who has administrative access to the database. No ads, no analytics, and your data is never sold.

Deleting it

Delete a job, or your whole account, in the app. It's removed straight away from the database and file storage. Copies may remain in our providers' backups for a short time.

What to leave out

Trozi doesn't need your home address or date of birth, so leave them off your CV. If your work involves security clearances or sensitive information, don't put those details into this or any online tool.
